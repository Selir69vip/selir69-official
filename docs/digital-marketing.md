# Digital Marketing — SELIR69

This document describes the digital marketing and online publishing principles used within the SELIR69 digital project.

The objective is to build consistent digital visibility through useful content, structured publishing, recognizable branding, and sustainable organic discovery.

---

## Digital Marketing Objectives

SELIR69 digital marketing activities focus on:

- Consistent digital branding
- Organic search visibility
- Content development
- Audience accessibility
- Multi-platform publishing
- Website discovery
- Content distribution
- Performance measurement
- Brand consistency

Digital marketing should support the broader web-development and publishing strategy rather than operate as an isolated activity.

---

## Content Strategy

Content should be created around clearly defined subjects and audience needs.

Before publishing, important considerations include:

- What information does the audience need?
- What is the primary subject?
- Is the information useful and accurate?
- Is the content easy to navigate?
- Does another existing page already cover the subject?
- Are related resources connected through appropriate links?

Each resource should have a clear purpose.

---

## Organic Search

Search visibility is supported through a combination of content quality and technical implementation.

Important areas include:

- Search intent
- Content relevance
- Website architecture
- Internal linking
- Metadata
- Crawlability
- Indexability
- Page performance
- Mobile usability

Organic search should not depend on excessive keyword repetition.

---

## Brand Consistency

SELIR69 should maintain consistent brand information across official digital properties.

Consistency may include:

- Project name
- Visual identity
- Website information
- Descriptions
- Messaging
- Official references

Consistent information makes digital resources easier for audiences to recognize.

---

## Digital Publishing

Content may be distributed through websites and relevant publishing platforms.

Each platform has different formats and community guidelines. Content should therefore be adapted to the platform rather than automatically duplicated without consideration for context.

Publishing practices should prioritize:

- Relevance
- Readability
- Accuracy
- Platform compatibility
- Audience value

---

## Social Media

Social platforms can support content discovery and communication.

Content should be adapted to each platform's format and policies.

Possible content formats include:

- Project updates
- Educational information
- Website resources
- Visual content
- Technical information
- Digital publishing updates

Social publishing should maintain consistent branding while avoiding repetitive or misleading messaging.

---

## SEO and Digital Marketing

SEO forms one component of the broader digital marketing strategy.

SEO helps users discover relevant resources through search engines, while digital marketing may also involve direct traffic, referrals, social discovery, content distribution, and brand recognition.

These activities should complement each other.

---

## Website as the Primary Resource

The official website should remain a primary source of project information.

External profiles can help audiences discover SELIR69, but important project information should be maintained through official resources whenever appropriate.

This reduces dependency on individual third-party platforms.

---

## Content Quality

Published information should aim to be:

- Useful
- Original
- Understandable
- Well structured
- Relevant
- Accessible
- Maintained

Publishing large quantities of low-value pages should not replace meaningful content development.

---

## Measurement

Digital marketing performance can be evaluated using relevant metrics.

Depending on the platform, these may include:

| Area | Example Measurement |
|---|---|
| Search | Organic visibility |
| Website | Visits and engagement |
| Content | Page performance |
| Referral | External traffic |
| Social | Audience interaction |
| Technical | Crawl and indexing health |

Metrics should be interpreted in context rather than treated as isolated indicators of success.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Digital marketing strategies should evolve based on performance, audience needs, platform changes, and website development.

Regular evaluation may include:

- Reviewing existing content
- Updating outdated information
- Improving internal links
- Evaluating search performance
- Reviewing website usability
- Identifying content gaps
- Monitoring technical issues

Continuous improvement is generally more sustainable than repeatedly replacing an established strategy without sufficient data.

---

## Responsible Publishing

SELIR69 digital publishing should follow the applicable rules of the platforms being used.

Content should avoid deceptive presentation, artificial engagement practices, misleading claims, and unnecessary repetition.

Long-term digital visibility depends on maintaining useful resources and consistent publishing standards.
